Privacy Policy

Churtern Play

Effective August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Churtern Play ("Churtern Play," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Churtern Play mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App"). By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account and contact information. We collect the information needed to create and manage an account, such as your username, name, email address, and password authentication data. Depending on the features you use, you may also choose to provide a profile photo, biography, phone number, birthdate, and state or region.

Collection and play information. We collect the games in your library and wishlist; favorites, rankings, for-sale status, and notes; game session dates, duration, player count, player names, scores, results, and other details you enter; and friend connections and library-sharing choices. Please obtain permission before entering another person's information and do not enter sensitive information in free-form notes.

Game and connector information. When you scan a barcode or search for a game, we process the barcode, search terms, game identifiers, and related game metadata. If you choose to connect a third-party account or convention service in the future, we may receive the profile, game collection, wishlist, registration, schedule, or other information you authorize that service to provide. The authorization screen for each connector will describe the requested access.

Camera data. Camera access is used to recognize a game barcode. Camera images are processed on your device and are not stored by Churtern Play. The recognized barcode is sent to our service for lookup.

Usage, device, and diagnostic information. Some builds may collect app interactions (such as screens viewed, buttons used, searches, scans, and feature usage), ad views and interactions, app version, device and operating-system type, language, time zone, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising or app-instance identifiers, crash reports, and performance logs. We use this information to operate the App, understand usage, fix problems, and plan improvements.

2. Where information comes from

We receive information directly from you, automatically from the App and our service providers, from other users when they connect or share with you, and from services you choose to connect.

For game identification and enrichment, the App currently uses BoardGameGeek, GameUPC, Barcode Lookup, and UPCitemdb. These sources primarily provide non-personal game information such as titles, publishers, release years, images, categories, mechanics, player counts, and BoardGameGeek identifiers. Future connectors may also provide information from game conventions or other game services. Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide libraries, wishlists, play tracking, social features, shared libraries, barcode lookup, game search, and connectors; personalize your experience; respond to support requests; prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; maintain and troubleshoot the App; measure performance and feature usage; develop future features; display and measure advertising; and comply with law and enforce our terms.

We may aggregate or de-identify information so it no longer reasonably identifies you, and use that information for analytics, research, and product planning.

4. When we disclose information

Service providers. We disclose information to vendors that host data, authenticate users, deliver app functionality, monitor reliability, provide analytics, support us, or perform other work on our behalf. They may use the information only for the services they provide to us and must protect it consistently with this Policy and applicable law.

Game-data providers. We send game search terms, game identifiers, or barcodes as needed to identify and enrich games. Current requests are handled through our server-side service. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, or birthdate to BoardGameGeek, GameUPC, Barcode Lookup, or UPCitemdb for these lookups. When a barcode is matched to a game, we may contribute the barcode and BoardGameGeek identifier to GameUPC to improve future lookups.

Other users. Your username, display name, profile image, biography, email address, and basic game statistics may currently be accessible to signed-in users so they can find and connect with you. Friends or people to whom you grant access may view the library information you share. Your phone number, birthdate, and state are not intended to appear in shared libraries.

Legal, safety, and business transfers. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or complete a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of the service. We will provide notice when legally required.

5. Advertising and analytics

The current beta does not include a third-party advertising or product analytics SDK. We plan to introduce usage analytics and may display paid advertisements. Before enabling a provider, we will update this Policy and the applicable app-store disclosures to identify material changes in the information collected and the purposes for which it is used.

Ads may be contextual (based on the screen or game content) or, where permitted and with any consent required by law or platform rules, personalized using device, advertising, usage, and approximate-location information. Advertising and analytics partners may receive device or app identifiers, IP address, device details, app interactions, ad views, and ad interactions. We do not provide those partners with your password or private game-session notes.

We do not sell personal information for money. Some privacy laws define the use of data for cross-context behavioral advertising as "selling" or "sharing." If we engage in that activity, we will provide the required notice and a way to opt out before or when it begins. You can also limit certain advertising identifiers through your device settings.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active or as reasonably needed to provide the App, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce agreements. Retention varies by the type of information and why we use it. Backup copies and security logs may remain for a limited period after deletion.

You can delete your account in Profile > Delete Account. Account deletion removes your profile, library, wishlist, sessions, friend relationships, and other account-linked content from active systems, subject to legal exceptions and limited backup retention. Shared game records and de-identified barcode-to-game mappings may remain because they are community game data and are not intended to identify you.

During a pre-release beta, account data may also be reset or removed as the service and data model change.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can review or change available profile and sharing settings in the App, disconnect supported connectors, revoke camera permission in device settings, adjust device advertising settings, and delete your account in the App.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of targeted advertising or certain sales or sharing. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right. To submit a request, use the developer contact shown on the App Store or Google Play listing. We may need to verify your identity before completing it.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws than your home jurisdiction. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Children

The App is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without legally required parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to this section, contact us through the developer contact shown on the app-store listing so we can investigate and delete it.

10. Entertainment-only service

Churtern Play is provided for personal entertainment and game-collection management. Game details, recommendations, randomizers, scores, statistics, convention information, and third-party content may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be treated as professional, medical, legal, financial, safety, or other expert advice.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy as the App changes. We will post the revised Policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required. Material changes affecting consent will not apply retroactively without any consent required by law.

Questions or privacy requests may be submitted using the developer contact information on the Churtern Play App Store or Google Play listing. Please do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in a request.
