About

Churtern Play

Churtern Play is a board game collection manager built for hobbyists who own physical games and play them socially. It exists because keeping track of a real shelf of games — what you own, what's on your wishlist, who won last time — is a problem software is genuinely good at solving.

What it's for

Scan a barcode or search by title to add a game and pull in its cover art, player count, playtime, and more. Keep a wishlist alongside your collection. When it's game night, use the built-in tools to pick a game, pick who goes first, and time turns or the whole session. Log who played and who won, and let those sessions build into stats over time.

Connect with friends to browse each other's collections and get suggestions for what to bring over.

Where it's headed

Churtern Play started as a web app and is currently in beta on iOS and Android. The web app, iOS app, and Android app share one backend and one collection, so your library stays in sync wherever you play.

Get in touch

Questions, feedback, or a bug to report? Reach out through the developer contact listed on the Churtern Play App Store or Google Play listing once the apps are live, or use the contact details provided wherever you signed up.
